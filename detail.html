Homemade Brioche

A weekend baking challenge to try at home!

A rich, buttery French bread perfect for breakfast or brunch.

Brioche Recipe

Instructions

  1. Combine dry ingredients. Add milk and eggs; mix with a dough hook for 3 minutes, then knead for 8-10 minutes.
  2. Pound cold butter into thin pieces.Incorporate gradually, then mix until elastic.
  3. Cover in a greased bowl; refrigerate for 12-16 hours.
  4. Shape six balls; stagger in a greased 9-by-5-inch loaf tin.
  5. Cover and rise for 1.5-2 hours, until dough reaches the rim.
  6. Apply glaze; bake at 177°C for 35-40 minutes. Cover loosely with foil if browning quickly.
  7. Rest in the tin for 10 minutes, then cool on a rack for at least an hour.

Ingredients

  • Bread flour: 423 g
  • Dry or instant yeast: 7 g
  • Sugar: 50 g
  • Salt: 1.5 teaspoons
  • Cold whole milk: 120 ml
  • Cold eggs: 3 large
  • Cold unsalted butter: 113 g
  • Glaze: 1 egg mixed with 15 ml milk

Recipe Info

Chilling Time: 12-16 hours

Rising Time: 1.5-2 hours

Baking Time: 35-40 minutes

Servings: 6

Difficulty: Intermediate
